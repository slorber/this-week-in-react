## 💸 Sponsor {#sponsor2}

**[The Fastest App: Benchmark your Android app](https://www.thefastest.app/?platform=android)**

Think your app launches fast? Put it to the test with The Fastest App. Android apps are tested on real devices in AWS Device Farm and ranked on a public leaderboard. A vision model analyses cold-start recordings frame by frame to identify the first recognisable screen after the splash screen (FCP) and when the main content is loaded and transitions settle (LCP). Results are averaged across runs, with CPU, RAM and FPS also measured. To get started, submit your Google Play app with at least 1,000 downloads, verify developer access and review the setup recording. You can also request automatic retesting and reports for each new release to track improvements and spot regressions.

The race starts [here](https://www.thefastest.app/?platform=android).
