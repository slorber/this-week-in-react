**[SurveyJS: UI Form Builder for React](https://surveyjs.io/?utm_source=this-week-in-react&utm_medium=email)**

Need complex forms in your React app? Building a custom form builder from scratch means handling drag-and-drop, logic, validation, and a UI for every setting. Then maintaining it all.

SurveyJS gives you an **embeddable React form builder** you can integrate into your app, including SaaS products. Let users create and update forms visually, without changing application code or redeploying.

Forms are stored as JSON, so you can version, update, and save them in your database. Extend the builder with custom inputs, validation rules, and property editors. Choose from built-in themes, create your own styles, or use [**theme adapters for MUI, Bootstrap, and shadcn/ui**](https://surveyjs.io/themes/theme-adapters?utm_source=this-week-in-react&utm_medium=email) to match your app's look and feel.

Self-hosted, with **full control over your data** and no per-end-user fees.
