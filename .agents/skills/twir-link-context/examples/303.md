## Shapes to recognize

- **Headline candidates**: "Redact" at the top of React, "Expo Desktop?" at the top of React Native. A short label, a few links, closed by a typed `—`.
- **`+` joins links**, on one line or at the end of a line: `…introducing-tanstack-redact +` continues on the next line.
- **Several links on one line**, separated by spaces, `+` or `?`: "framer motion 14.5 ? … ? …", "Expo Exact? … + … + …", "crnl … …", the two WebKit posts, "swc next … …".
- **Short hints before a link**: "legend spark", "action sheet v11", "nitro node", "crnl", "shaduocn", "calculus", "motherload", "bongo"… They name or describe what's behind the link. Read the link: a hint isn't a title.
- **Questions**: "Expo Desktop?", "framer motion 14.5 ?", "What is Charlie Cheever working on???", "nitro alpha?", "keyframer inspector?", and `???` after a URL.
- **`# …` notes after a link**: "# Did Jamie publish something better?", "# submission".
- **Group labels that aren't headlines**: "Seb notes:", "Seb last minute:" (late additions, not at the top of the section).
- **The same news on two lines**: "nitro node" points to an X post in the main list, and to a GitHub repo under "Seb last minute:".
- **Index pages instead of posts**: `https://www.fabwaseem.com/blog ???`, `https://pnpm.io/blog`.
- **A reader submission with a private Gmail link.**
- **Items already written** among raw links: "📦 Expo Desktop 1.0" (with a `#` question), "📦 maplint".
- **Noise**: empty `- ` bullets, a line of links outside any list ("swc next"), tracking parameters (`?s=20`, `?s=09`), an `http://` link, `---` closing each section, a TODO in the Intro, an empty Fun section.
